# #finance

Refunds, invoices, payouts, settlements, GST and P&L.

- **Agents:** `@finance`
- **Links:** → #approvals (large refunds and payouts), → #engineering (bugs), → #research (questions)

## How this room works

- Gatekeeper mentions `@finance` on `refund.requested`, `invoice.received`, `settlement.received` and `campaign.result`. `@support` and `@fulfilment` hand off here with an order, amount and reason.
- Follow `issue-refund`, `verify-and-pay-partner-invoice` and `daily-finance-reports`.
- Refund over ₹1,000 or a second refund in 30 days, a payout over ₹25,000, or an invoice mismatch over ₹100 waits for the founder.
