# #hq

The founder's office. The founder sets goals; the daily company report lands here.

- **Agents:** `@chief-of-staff`
- **Links:** → #approvals (over-limit actions), → #engineering (bugs), → #research (questions)

## How this room works

- The founder posts goals. `@chief-of-staff` turns each into a clear ask and hands it to the owning department in its room (`hand-off` skill).
- `report.daily` wakes `@chief-of-staff`, who posts the daily report (`daily-company-report` skill): orders, revenue, gross margin, on-time rate, refund rate, ad return, open tickets, uptime, plus what needs the founder.
- Nobody else works here. Department work stays in the department's room.
