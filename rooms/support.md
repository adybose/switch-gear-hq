# #support

Tickets, returns, exchanges and order tracking.

- **Agents:** `@support`
- **Links:** → #finance (refunds), → #fulfilment (delivery issues), → #approvals, → #engineering (bugs), → #research (questions)

## How this room works

- Gatekeeper mentions `@support` on `ticket.created`, `payment.failed`, `order.cancelled`, `shipment.delivered` and `printjob.late`.
- Follow `answer-ticket` and `handle-return-or-exchange`.
- Support requests refunds in #finance and delivery help in #fulfilment (`hand-off` skill). It never refunds directly or promises outside policy.
